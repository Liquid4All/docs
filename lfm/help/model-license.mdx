## Overview

The LFM Open License is based on **Apache 2.0**, granting broad, royalty-free, perpetual rights to use, modify, and distribute our models and their derivatives. The key difference from Apache 2.0 is a **commercial use threshold**: free commercial use is limited to companies with annual revenue under $10 million USD.

## Using the Models

You can use, modify, fine-tune, and distribute the models for any purpose, including commercial products and services.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Do I have to open-source my modifications?">
No. The license has no copyleft requirement. You can keep your fine-tuned models proprietary.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who owns what?">
Liquid AI retains ownership of the base models. You own your modifications, but use of derivative models remains subject to this license.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Commercial Use

Companies with annual revenue under **$10 million USD** can use the models at no cost for commercial purposes.

If your company's annual revenue exceeds **$10 million USD**, your right to use the models commercially under this license ends. Contact [sales@liquid.ai](mailto:sales@liquid.ai) to obtain a commercial license.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What counts as commercial use?">
Any use for direct or indirect commercial advantage or monetary compensation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does the threshold apply if I distribute the models to customers?">
The threshold applies separately to each company using the models commercially. Your company’s revenue determines your right to use and distribute the models commercially. If a downstream customer uses the models commercially, that customer’s own revenue determines whether it needs a commercial license. A customer with annual revenue over **$10 million USD** needs a commercial license from Liquid AI.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get a commercial license that covers my customers or end users?">
The open license doesn’t automatically cover a downstream customer’s commercial use. If you are interested in a custom commercial license covering your customers or end users, contact [sales@liquid.ai](mailto:sales@liquid.ai) to discuss available options.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Research & Nonprofit Use

Qualified nonprofits (501(c)(3) or equivalent) can use the models for non-commercial or research purposes with **no revenue threshold**.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What qualifies as a nonprofit?">
Organizations exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the U.S. tax code, or equivalent charitable or educational organizations in other jurisdictions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What about for-profit schools and education companies?">
For-profit organizations using the models commercially remain subject to the commercial-use threshold.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Distribution Requirements

When distributing the models or derivatives, you must:

- Include a copy of the license
- Retain all copyright and attribution notices
- Include the NOTICE file if one is provided with the model
- Mark any files you have modified

<Accordion title="Can I say that my product uses Liquid AI models?">
Yes. You may say that your product uses Liquid AI models, but the license doesn’t allow broader use of Liquid AI’s trademarks or branding.
</Accordion>

## Termination

The license terminates **automatically and immediately** if you violate any of its terms. Upon termination, you must stop using the models and delete all copies.

## Comparison to Apache 2.0

The LFM Open License is based directly on Apache 2.0. Other than the differences below, the licenses are identical.

- **Conditional License Grants (Sections 2 & 3):** The grants for both copyright and patent rights are explicitly "subject to" the commercial use limitation.
- **Commercial Use Limitation (Section 5):** This is the most significant change. The LFM Open License adds a condition that limits free commercial use to companies with under $10 million in annual revenue.
- **Specific Licensor:** The "Licensor" is explicitly defined as "Liquid AI, Inc." for clarity.
- **Termination Clause (Section 11):** The license terminates automatically if you fail to comply with its terms (namely the commercial use restriction).
