LFM2.5-VL-1.6B is a general-purpose vision-language model for image understanding, visual reasoning, and OCR. It is a refreshed version of LFM2-VL-1.6B, built on the LFM2.5-1.2B-Base language backbone with a SigLIP2 NaFlex shape-optimized 400M vision encoder. Compared to LFM2-VL-1.6B, it improves instruction following, multilingual vision understanding, and results on multi-image inputs, high-resolution images, and OCR. For the most capable vision model, see [LFM2.5-VL-3B](/lfm/models/lfm25-vl-3b).
