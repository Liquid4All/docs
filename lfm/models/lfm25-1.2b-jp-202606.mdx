LFM2.5-1.2B-JP-202606 is Liquid AI's general-purpose Japanese chat model. It improves on [LFM2.5-1.2B-JP](/lfm/models/lfm25-1.2b-jp) in knowledge, instruction following, math, code, and tool use. It is recommended for agentic workflows, tool use, structured outputs, bilingual English-Japanese assistants, and on-device personal assistants. It is not recommended for knowledge-intensive tasks, and it works best with clear, explicit instructions that define the task, expected behavior, and output format.
