<h2>LFM3 Models &nbsp;&nbsp;<Badge shape="rounded" icon="circle-check" color="green" size="lg">Latest release</Badge></h2>

<Info>LFM3-VL pairs the LFM3 language backbone with a new in-house vision encoder that combines convolution and attention. It brings video understanding to LFM vision models for the first time, and reasons step by step before answering.</Info>

## LFM2.5 Models

<Info>LFM2.5-VL models are refreshed versions of LFM2-VL, built on new LFM2.5 language backbones (LFM2.5-350M, LFM2.5-1.2B-Base, and LFM2.5-2.6B) with SigLIP2 NaFlex vision encoders. They improve instruction following and multilingual vision understanding, and support function calling.</Info>
